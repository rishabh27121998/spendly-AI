Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when creating an account, as well as the expense data you log — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate the Service: to authenticate your account, store and display your expenses, and generate summaries and breakdowns of your spending. We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and are only accessible to you through your authenticated account. We retain your data for as long as your account remains active, and you may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers to host the application and store data. These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. We do not share your personal or financial data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on the Spendly website.
